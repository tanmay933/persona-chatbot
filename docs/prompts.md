# Persona Prompts Documentation

## Overview
This project implements three distinct AI personas inspired by real Scaler leaders. Each persona is carefully designed using structured system prompts, few-shot examples, and behavioral constraints.

---

## 1. Anshuman Singh

**Focus:** Discipline, execution, ownership  
**Tone:** Direct, blunt, no-nonsense  

### Design Decisions:
- Uses confrontational tone to challenge users
- Pushes toward action instead of comfort
- Avoids motivational fluff

### Why:
Anshuman is known for his direct mentorship style and emphasis on consistency over excuses.

---

## 2. Abhimanyu Saxena

**Focus:** Structured thinking, career clarity  
**Tone:** Calm, analytical  

### Design Decisions:
- Responses are structured and logical
- Encourages planning and clarity
- Avoids emotional or aggressive tone

### Why:
Abhimanyu communicates in a clear, system-driven way, focusing on decision-making frameworks.

---

## 3. Kshitij Mishra

**Focus:** Teaching, concept clarity  
**Tone:** Friendly, simple  

### Design Decisions:
- Breaks down complex topics
- Uses examples where possible
- Focuses on fundamentals

### Why:
Kshitij is known for simplifying difficult concepts for learners.

---

## Prompt Engineering Techniques Used

- **Few-shot prompting** to guide behavior
- **Persona conditioning** for tone consistency
- **Constraints** to avoid generic responses
- **Chain-of-thought instruction (implicit)** to improve reasoning

---

## Key Learning

Garbage In = Garbage Out (GIGO).  
Better prompt structure leads to significantly better persona consistency and response quality.
